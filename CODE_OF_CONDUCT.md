# Community guidelines

This project welcomes students, developers, designers, and reviewers who want to make Portal more reliable.

Please:

- be respectful and patient;
- discuss the work rather than the person;
- give specific, useful feedback;
- make room for people with different levels of experience;
- protect student privacy; and
- accept a maintainer's decision when it is made to protect users, release signing, or the official portal.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not accepted.

If a conversation becomes unproductive, maintainers may lock it, remove content, or restrict participation. Serious conduct concerns can be reported privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile.
